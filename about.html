BRIGHTSIDE // ARCHIVAL NETWORK

SYSTEM NOTES

NODE 04

Archival purpose

Brightside maintains controlled copies of records acquired, recovered, or submitted through its network. This terminal provides retrieval access to indexed material only.

Record status

PUBLIC — available to authorised archive users.

RESTRICTED — indexed, but access requires an archive key.

WITHDRAWN — removed from normal circulation while retained in the archive.

UNVERIFIED — source or provenance has not been independently confirmed.

CORRUPTED — indexed copy is incomplete or damaged.

Archive contents are not intended for external distribution.
